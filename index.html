About

Carter Haugen is a trumpet player, producer, and arranger based in Lansing, Michigan. He is currently attending the Jazz Studies program at Michigan State University under the direction of Professor Rodney Whitaker.

When on the horn, he works between hard bop, modal jazz, and fusion. He also produces a wide variety of genres from his home studio.

Carter also organizes local live music events and manages jazz booking at Ryan Records in East Lansing, contributing to a growing live music scene.
